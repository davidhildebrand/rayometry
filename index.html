Measure & explore

Protractor: drag its center or transparent face to move it, the outer teal handle to rotate, and the gold arm to measure. Align the baseline with the surface normal for incidence or refraction.

Dashed extensions continue the internal direction beyond the flat face, ignoring exit refraction. They are construction lines, not additional light rays.

Distances are in mm; the ruler reads unsigned length. Drag either ruler end to measure, or drag its middle to move it. Measurements remain available in Inspect.
